# TINY RED OOZE

***Tier 1 Skulk***  
*A small moving mound of translucent flaming red slime.*  
**Motivações & Táticas:** Blaze, camouflage

> **Dificuldade:** 11 | **Limiares:** 5/None | **PV:** 2 | **Estresse:** 1  
> **ATQ:** -1 | **Ooze Appendage:** Melee | 1d4+2 mag
